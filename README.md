# Coach!

Know your next move.

A gym tracker whose main screen answers the one question that actually matters:
**what weight do I put on the bar for the next set?**

- Log one honest set on a lift and Coach estimates your one-rep max (Epley, adjusted
  for reps left in the tank).
- Pick a goal — **Stronger** (3–5 reps at 82–87%) or **Bigger** (6–12 at 67–78%) —
  and it prescribes weight, reps, sets and rest.
- Log what you *actually* did. Hit the top of the range with a rep spare and the
  weight goes up; miss the bottom and it backs off; anything else holds.
- Every number snaps to plates you actually own.

Five session types — Push, Pull, Legs, Upper, Lower — each with its own colour.
You choose what you trained; the app never dictates a rotation.

Everything is stored on your device. No account, no server, no tracking.

## Install on your phone

Open the site in **Safari** on iOS, then Share → **Add to Home Screen**.
It gets an icon and runs fullscreen, offline included.

## Run locally

```
python3 -m http.server 8777
```

Then open http://127.0.0.1:8777
